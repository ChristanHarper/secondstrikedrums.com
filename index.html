// FAQ

Good to know

Something else? Just ask.

Q.01Where are you?

Dickson County, Tennessee, just west of Nashville. Local drop-off and pickup by appointment. Call or text 615-736-1869 to set a time.

Q.02Are parts and heads included?

No. Heads, parts, materials, and shipping are extra unless they're on your quote.

Q.03Is there an assessment fee?

A $25 fee may apply for detailed inspections or custom estimates. We confirm it first, and it can be credited toward the work.

Q.04What if you find more problems?

We call you before doing anything outside the original quote. Nothing extra without your OK.

Q.05Not local. Can I ship it?

Yes. Ship it to us, and return shipping is charged at actual label cost. Pack it well, and use tracking and insurance on valuable drums.

Q.06Anything you don't do?

No machining, bearing-edge cutting, or structural shell repairs right now.
